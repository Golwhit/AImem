## Boundaries

- Unknown extracted patterns remain available for review; collection does not
  admit them into Layer 0 or approve them for training.
- Layer 1 only excludes known impossibilities. Further layers are optional,
  not a prerequisite for integration.
- Memory capture continues independently of training approval.
- Preserve original memories and provenance; consolidation and thought
  progression do not yet authorize rewriting, deleting, or training on them.
- Growth, graph promotion, and generation counts alone do not demonstrate
  understanding or thought development.
